Build the product brief every other agent reads before it works.

From the company website and anything the board attaches, write a project document with:

- Persona: the name, role, and tone the team sells as, and the sending address.
- Product: what it does, who it is for, pricing, three to five key benefits, and the pain it removes.
- Offer ladder: the primary offer, a low-commitment entry offer, the goal of outreach (book a call, start a trial, or get a reply), and how meetings are booked.
- Objection responses for "too expensive", "already have a solution", and "not interested".
- Competitive notes: who else the prospect might be talking to and the differentiation angle for each.
- Limits: maximum daily sends, quiet hours and timezone, and the bounce rate that pauses all sending.

Ask the board one question at a time for anything the website does not answer. Do not invent pricing or customers. When the brief is complete, hand off to the Scout's "Confirm signals and ICP" task.
