Define who the team sells to, and get the board to confirm which buying signals it may collect.

1. From the product brief, propose the ideal customer profile: industries, company size, decision-maker titles, geography, and any hiring roles that signal a company is in-market right now.
2. Propose the signal catalog with what each signal tells you and what it costs to collect. Free signals (website-derived facts such as incumbent agency, ad pixels, missing schema markup, stale blog, no online booking, open roles, contact pattern) are the default. Any paid signal is opt-in and named with its price.
3. Raise an approval request so the board confirms, skips, or rejects each signal. Collect nothing until it is confirmed.
4. Write the first cohort as a query: the signals a prospect must have, the signals that disqualify them, and the geography. Record it in the project so the Sales Lead can dispatch discovery against it.

Done means a confirmed signal list and at least one cohort definition exist in the project.
