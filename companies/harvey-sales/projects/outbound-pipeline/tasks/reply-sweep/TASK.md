Check for new replies and bounces on every open conversation.

Classify each reply, advance its stage, cancel queued sends for anyone who replied, opted out, or bounced, and draft responses for the intents that get one. Escalate angry, legal, or compliance replies to the board without answering them. Submit drafts for approval. Report the bounce rate against the threshold; if it is exceeded, ask the Sales Lead to pause sending.
